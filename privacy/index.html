Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026 · Last updated November 3, 2026

This policy describes how SoundLogic IO handles information processed by Hermes, a private Google Chat assistant operated for internal business use by Iovanne Music Academy. Hermes is not affiliated with Google.

Information Hermes processes

  • Chat content: message text, replies, thread context, reactions, and related event information from spaces where Hermes is installed or otherwise authorized to observe.
  • Message metadata: sender and space identifiers, timestamps, thread identifiers, membership information, and information needed to deliver a response.
  • Operational information: error details, delivery state, configuration, and diagnostic logs needed to operate and secure the service.
  • Context and memory: conversation context or memory may be retained when those Hermes features are enabled. The exact behavior is controlled by the deployment configuration.

How information is used

Hermes uses this information to receive Google Chat events, decide whether a message merits a response, generate and deliver responses, maintain requested context, troubleshoot failures, prevent abuse, and protect the service. The current deployment is intended to use an operator-controlled model endpoint. If the operator changes the model or adds an external provider, the operator should update this policy and review that provider's handling terms.

Where information is processed

Google Workspace and Google Chat provide the source events and delivery APIs. Google Cloud Pub/Sub transports event messages to the operator-controlled Hermes host. Hermes and its configured model endpoint then process the content. The policy site itself does not receive Chat content and does not use analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking scripts.

Sharing

SoundLogic IO does not sell Chat content or use it for advertising. Information may be processed by Google Workspace services and by infrastructure or model services deliberately configured by the operator to run Hermes. Access is limited to the operator, authorized administrators, and authorized personnel who need it to operate or support the service.

Retention

There is no fixed retention period stated by this policy. Google Workspace retention is governed by the organization's Workspace and Vault settings. Pub/Sub delivery data is retained according to the configured subscription behavior. Local logs, conversation context, and memory are retained according to the Hermes deployment configuration and may be removed by the operator. Users should avoid sending information that is more sensitive than the work requires.

Security

The operator uses access controls and keyless or managed credentials where supported, but no networked service can guarantee absolute security. Report suspected unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, or a security issue promptly to michael@iovannemusicacademy.com.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Hermes, its model, its integrations, or its retention practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions, contact michael@iovannemusicacademy.com.
