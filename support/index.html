Hermes support

Last updated November 3, 2026

Hermes is an internal Google Chat assistant for authorized members of the Iovanne Music Academy Workspace. It is operated by SoundLogic IO.

Using Hermes

  1. Add the Hermes Chat app to a direct message or a Google Chat space.
  2. For a direct interaction, mention @Hermes and include your request.
  3. In spaces where proactive watching is enabled, Hermes may observe ordinary messages and respond only when its instructions determine that it has something useful to contribute.

If Hermes does not respond

  • Confirm that Hermes is still listed as a member or app in the space.
  • For a guaranteed interaction test, mention @Hermes.
  • Allow a short delay for Google Chat event delivery and model processing.
  • Check whether the message is in a space covered by the current deployment and Workspace authorization.
  • Do not include credentials, tokens, or other secrets in a support request.

Contact

For access, privacy, or operational problems, contact michael@iovannemusicacademy.com. Include the approximate time, space name, and a short description of the problem, but omit sensitive message content.
